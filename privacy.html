Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

1. Whose data is involved

ES Lesson Uploader ("the App") is a personal, single-user tool. The only person whose data it touches is its owner. It collects no data from anyone else, ever.

2. What the App accesses

Through TikTok's API, with the owner's explicit authorization, the App accesses:

  • Basic profile information of the owner's TikTok account (user.info.basic scope);
  • The ability to upload videos to the owner's own draft inbox (video.upload scope).

3. What is stored, and where

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. No data is transmitted to any third party other than TikTok itself. The App has no server, no database, no analytics, and no tracking.

4. Data sharing and sale

No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

5. Data deletion

Authorization can be revoked at any time from the owner's TikTok app under Settings → Security → Apps and services, and local tokens can be deleted at any time by deleting the token file.
